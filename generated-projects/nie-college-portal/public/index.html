Academic Programs

Choose from a diverse range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral research degree programs.

Undergraduate (B.E. / B.Tech)

Full-time 4-year engineering degree programs designed to build strong fundamentals and practical engineering skills.

  • Computer Science & Engg
  • Information Science & Engg
  • Electronics & Communication
  • Mechanical & Civil Engg

Postgraduate (M.Tech / MCA)

Advanced 2-year professional programs focused on specialized technical domains, research, and industry applications.

  • M.Tech in Structural Engineering
  • M.Tech in Computer Science
  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Doctoral Research (Ph.D.)

Research-oriented programs fostering cutting-edge discoveries, patents, and publications across all engineering disciplines.

  • Full-time & Part-time Ph.D.
  • VTU Recognized Research Centers
  • Sponsored Research Grants
