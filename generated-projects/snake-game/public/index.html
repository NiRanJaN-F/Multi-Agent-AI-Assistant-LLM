How to Play CyberSnake

Objective: Guide your cyber-snake across the grid to consume glowing data nodes and energy orbs.

Scoring: Each normal node grants 10 points multiplied by your speed tier. Special pulsing nodes grant bonus points and temporary multiplier boosts!

Game Modes:

  • Classic: Standard gameplay, screen wrapping or wall crashes depending on settings.
  • Obstacle Maze: Internal cyber-barriers spawn on the grid. Avoid them at all costs!
  • Hyper Speed: Extreme acceleration mode for seasoned reflexes.
