How To Play

1. Objective: Eat glowing food pellets to grow longer and score points.

2. Controls: Use Arrow keys or W, A, S, D keys to change direction.

3. Danger: Avoid crashing into the outer boundaries or the snake's own tail.

4. Pause: Press Spacebar anytime to pause your game session.
